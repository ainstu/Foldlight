怎么合作How we work

  1. 1

    发资料Brief

    你发资料和要求，说明用途：印刷、邮件、展会、汇报还是上线。Send your files and brief, and say what it's for: print, email, a trade show, a presentation or the web.

  2. 2

    确认范围Scope

    我确认工作内容和交付时间，缺的资料一次列给你。I confirm the scope and date, and list anything missing in one go.

    付 50% 定金开工50% deposit to start
  3. 3

    样稿Proof

    先出约三成的样稿，带水印，用来定风格和方向。A watermarked proof at about 30%, to settle style and direction.

    可调整风格方向Style can still change
  4. 4

    修改Revisions

    样稿确认后，在确认的范围内改到满意为止。After the proof is approved, revisions within scope until you're happy.

    超出范围另行报价Out of scope: quoted separately
  5. 5

    交付Delivery

    交终稿；确认并付尾款后，交可编辑源文件。Final files; once approved and paid, the editable source files.

    付尾款 → 源文件Balance paid → source files

交付周期一般 2 到 3 天，复杂项目一周以内。Turnaround is usually 2 to 3 days; complex projects take up to a week.

不含产品拍摄、印刷和寄送、图纸的工程审核和签字盖章，以及需另外购买的商用字体和图片授权。Not included: product photography, printing and shipping, engineering review, signing or stamping of drawings, and any commercial font or image licences that need to be bought.

项目过程How it went

客户给的What the client sent
一份基金申报书里的 10 张框图截图，分辨率低，不少字是乱码或错字，要求全部改成 Visio 可编辑。Screenshots of ten diagrams from a grant proposal: low resolution, with garbled and misspelt text throughout, to be rebuilt as editable Visio files.
难点The hard part
有些字和公式已经认不出来，乱改就会改错学术内容。Some words and formulas were no longer legible, and guessing wrong would change the science.
怎么做What I did
明确的错别字直接改，比如“送代”改“迭代”、“合架”改“台架”；只能按上下文推断的 15 处列成清单请客户确认；另外指出原图里 5 处数据和曲线对不上的地方，由客户决定改不改。关键数字一个不动。Obvious typos I fixed directly, such as 送代 to 迭代 (iteration). The 15 spots I could only infer from context went to the client as a checklist to confirm, and I flagged five places where the original's numbers and curves didn't match, for the client to decide. No key figure was changed.
交付Delivered
10 张 Visio 源文件和 PDF，文字、箭头和公式都可以直接改。Ten Visio source files plus PDF, with every label, arrow and formula editable.
