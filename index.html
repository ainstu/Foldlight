怎么合作How we work

  1. 你发资料和要求，说明用途：印刷、发邮件、展会、汇报还是上线。You send your files and brief, and tell me what it's for: print, email, a trade show, a presentation or the web.
  2. 我确认工作内容和交付时间，缺的资料一次列给你；确认后付 50% 定金开工。I confirm the scope and delivery date and list anything missing in one go. Work starts once the 50% deposit is paid.
  3. 先出样稿，大约完成三成，带水印，用来定风格和方向。You get a first proof at about 30% complete, watermarked, to settle the style and direction.
  4. 样稿阶段可调整方向；样稿确认后含 2 轮修改，错字和参数修正不计次数，超出另计。The direction can still change at the proof stage. Once the proof is approved, 2 rounds of revisions are included. Typos and data corrections don't count as a round. Anything beyond that is quoted separately.
  5. 交终稿；你确认后付尾款，付清后交可编辑源文件。I deliver the final files. After you approve them and pay the balance, you receive the editable source files.

交付周期一般 2 到 3 天，复杂项目一周以内。Turnaround is usually 2 to 3 days; complex projects take up to a week.

不含产品拍摄、印刷和寄送、图纸的工程审核和签字盖章，以及需另外购买的商用字体和图片授权。Not included: product photography, printing and shipping, engineering review, signing or stamping of drawings, and any commercial font or image licences that need to be bought.
